How it works

Oyan suggests bike loops around Barcelona, calculated in advance to get out of town quickly, avoid traffic lights and main roads, and ride through forest or by the water.

What is Oyan?

Oyan suggests bike loops calculated in advance from about 860 starting points in the province of Barcelona: neighbourhoods, villages and stations. You choose a duration, from 45 min to 6 h, and a pace; Oyan shows the best-rated loop, ready to download as a GPX file. It is free, with no account and no ads.

How are the loops chosen?

For each start, duration and pace, Oyan compares about twenty loops drawn on OpenStreetMap roads and keeps the best-rated one. The score goes up with cycle paths, forest and water. It goes down with traffic, traffic lights, dirt tracks, main roads and industrial areas.

How is the time estimated?

The time is calculated for your pace, including hills, town and traffic lights (about 10 seconds per light), without breaks. If the nearest start is a few minutes from home, that ride is added to the total time.

What are the paces, and what is FTP?

Relaxed is about 25 km/h on the flat, riding solo (FTP ≈ 2.3 W/kg), Moderate about 29 km/h (FTP ≈ 3.0 W/kg) and Sporty about 32 km/h (FTP ≈ 3.8 W/kg). FTP is the power you can hold for about an hour, in watts per kilo: if you know yours, it helps you choose.

What does climbing (D+) mean?

Climbing, or D+, is the total number of metres climbed over the loop, all climbs added up. Oyan also lists every climb of more than 20 m, with its length and steepness.

How do I follow the loop on the road?

Download the GPX file and open it on your bike computer (Garmin, Wahoo…) or in a free navigation app that reads GPX, such as OsmAnd or Organic Maps. Oyan has no built-in navigation.

Can I start from a train station?

Yes: some starts are Rodalies or FGC stations that you reach by train with your bike. Check timetables and bike rules before you go.

Have the loops been checked on the ground?

Not all of them. Before you go, Oyan flags anything that may bother you: probably unpaved tracks, traffic lights, main roads, industrial areas. A road may have changed since the calculation: keep an eye on the road and the signs.

What personal data does Oyan use?

Your location stays in your browser and is never sent. The text of an address search is sent only to the Photon service, at the moment you search. No account, no tracking cookies, no analytics.
